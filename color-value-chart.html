Color Value Chart

Reading the Chart

The six “arms” of differently colored hexes [each hex of which contains a differently colored square] show the ColorValue number of that color magical unit [represented by the square] when present in that color hex. For example, the ColorValue of a Green magical unit when in a Blue hex is “1.” This number indicates three things in different instances: 1) it is the number of Movement Points such a magical unit will have to expend in order to exit that color hex [unassisted by a same-color Sorcerer] when that hex is the first hex exited in the Movement and Conjuration Phase. 2) It is the die roll range for attrition purposes when attrition is called for in that color hex when the magical unit is not accompanied by a same-color Sorcerer. An attrition die roll equal to, or less than the number shown indicates that that unit loses one step. 3) It is the Color Augmentation of a force containing that color magical unit in that color hex.
