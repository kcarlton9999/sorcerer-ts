# Sorcerer Documentation

## [18.0] TWO-PLAYER SCENARIOS

It is suggested that Players use Scenario 18.1 for their first game. Note that the two-Player scenarios may be converted to multi-Player scenarios by having other Players assume the roles of the subordinate Sorcerers.

#### [18.1] THE RIVALRY OF BALT AND DNAI
In the 37th cycle of the First Age, Dnai grew fearful of the growing power and popularity of Balt He resolved to humiliate (or eliminate) Balt by the conversion of the White realms, thus drawing his enemy into a confrontation in which (Dnai assumed) he would be able to shatter Balt's power base. And shower as much glory as possible on the wonderful and awesome Dnai. He almost succeeded.

**[18.11]** STARTING FORCES
**First Player** (Dnai):
One Green-Orange-Purple Sorcerer (Dnai)   
One Orange Sorcerer   
Three human infantry   
One Orange (8) fortress   
Deployment: Within three hexes of Cessa-Abirran (2017).   

**Second Player** (Balt):   
One Blue-Yellow Sorcerer (Balt)   
One Blue Sorcerer   
One Yellow Sorcerer   
Five human infantry   
One Yellow (4) fortress 
  
Deployment: Within three hexes of Amma-Avattar (0711).   

**[18.12]** VICTORY CONDITIONS   
As described in 15.0. Judged at the end of **eight** Game-Turns.

SPECIAL RULES:   
**[18.13]** Only the multi-colored Sorcerers may use teleportation.

#### [18.2] USSA, BALT’S SON, CHALLENGES OLD DNAI
In the 45th cycle of the First Age, Balt disappeared under suspicious circumstances. Ussa, who had surpassed his father in powers, immediately accused Dnai of murder before the Convocation of Citylords. The Citylords, ever equivocal in their deliberations concerning Sorcerers, did nothing. Ussa, with a temper as bad as his father&apos;s was good, immediately set about committing provocations in the White Realms, not to mention assaulting Dnai&apos;s strongholds. Just as Ussa was about to do something indescribable to the defeated Dnai, Bait returned from his torm-fishing trip in the Blue universe. For his precipitous action in taking the law into his own hands, Ussa was fined 3,456 silver leients and Dnai was awarded 2,345 gold leients as compensation. Ussa, of course, paid none of this. But he did have to answer to his father (worse luck for Ussa).

**[18.21]** STARTING FORCES   
**First Player** (Ussa)   
One Blue-Yellow-Red Sorcerer (Ussa)   
One Blue Sorcerer   
One Yellow Sorcerer   
One Red Sorcerer   
Seven human infantry   
One Yellow (8) fortress within three hexes of Amma-Avattar (0711)   
One Blue (4) fortress within three hexes of Ivor-Vorranna (1016)   
One Red (4) fortress within three hexes of Bannorka (0507)   
Deployment: Sorcerers in fortresses; human infantry in, or within three hexes of fortresses. Alternatively, set-up as per 16.0.

**Second Player** (Dnai)   
One Green-Orange-Purple Sorcerer (Dnai)   
One Green Sorcerer   
One Orange Sorcerer   
One Purple Sorcerer   
Five human infantry   
One Orange (4) fortress within three hexes of Cessa-Abirran (2017)   
One Green (4) fortress within three hexes of Lyann-Dritta (1205)   
Ore Purple (4) fortress within three hexes of Riddella-Bes (1710)   

Deployment: Sorcerers in fortresses; human infantry in. or within three hexes of fortresses. Prime Sorcerer in Orange Fortress. Alternatively, set-up as per 16.0.   

**[18.22]** VICTORY CONDITIONS   
As per 15.0. Game is a maximum of ten Game-Turns. unless Balt returns early from his fishing trip (see special rule 18.23).

SPECIAL RULES:   
**[18.23] Ending the Game:**   
Victory is judged at the end of ten complete Game-Turns, unless Balt comes home from his trip earlier. At the end of each Game-Turn, from the Sixth Game-Turn onward, roll the die. If the number rolled is less than half the Game-Turn number in which the die is rolled, Balt returns and the Game is over immediately. For example, at the end of Game-Turn Seven, a roll of "3” or less ends the game.

**[18.24]** Only the multi-color Sorcerers may use teleportation.

#### [18.3] THE UPRISING OF THE CITY STATES
In the 56th Cycle of the First Age, Fain proclaimed direct rule over the cities of Dernma and Ridella-Bes. No one would have objected had not Fain so rigorously and repressively implemented his usurpation of the Citylords&apos; authority. He doubled taxes, erected statues of himself in every square, and generally made a nuisance of himself. His final excess came on Planter’s Day, when he decreed that the heretofore joyously bacchanalian festival day was now to be spent in ascetic contemplation of the majesty of Fain and the exercise of correct Fainetic thought. The populations of the two cities revolted; Fain reacted with a heavy hand, and the armies of the other eight cities moved to free Demma and Riddella-Bes from the depredations of the megalomaniacal Fain. The Sorcerers Noli and Chir, seeing the justice of the cities&apos; struggle (and sensing an opportunity to grab off some of Fain&apos;s power), came to their aid. Upon the liberation of Demma, Fain had a rare attack of good sense and capitulated. All were reasonably pleased with the result (except Noli and Chir, who wound up with no more substantial reward for their efforts than a certificate of appreciation). Planter&apos;s Day was held a few weeks late, but with more than the usual fervor. Even Fain enjoyed himself.

**[18.31]** STARTING FORCES   
**First Player** (City States)   
One Blue Sorcerer (Chir)   
One Red Sorcerer (Noli)   
Fourteen human infantry   
One Blue (2) fortress within three hexes of Kel-Fannyan (0416)   
One Red (2) fortress within- three hexes of Bannorka (0507)   

Deployment: Human infantry in Gray cities, except Demma and Riddella-Bes. At least one
human infantry unit in each of the eight cities. Sorcerers in fortresses.

**Second Player** (Fain)   
One Green-Purple Sorcerer   
Four human infantry   
One Purple (4) fortress within three hexes of Ridella-Bes (1710)   
Five Purple demonic infantry   

Deployment: Two human infantry in Demma (1313); two in Riddella-Bes (1710). Sorcerer in
fortress; demonic infantry within three hexes of fortress.   

**[18.32]** VICTORY CONDITIONS   
The City States win if they capture either Demma or Riddella-Bes, or if the Purple fortress is destroyed. Fain wins if he avoids the City State victory condition for six Game-Turns, or if he destroys one of the Enemy fortresses.

SPECIAL RULES:   

**[18.33]** The Red Sorcerer must be designated as the Prime Sorcerer of the City States Player. Only the Red Sorcerer and the Green-Purple Sorcerer may teleport.   

**[18.34]** &ldquo;Capture&rdquo; of a city for victory is defined as having one or more full strength, unpinned human infantry units in the city hex for two consecutive Joint Combat Phases.

#### [18.4] THE WAR FOR DOMINANCE

At the dawn of the Second Age. the fragile structure of agreements and unstated compacts
that had kept the growing Sorcerer community relatively at peace, broke down ever the Incident of the Red Glove. The Red Glove had been fabricated by artisan Banforl for an unknown Sorcerer-client. Supposedly it would have allowed a non-Red Sorcerer to conjure in Red magic. Whether it would have worked or not will never be known, since the Glove, Banforl and his client were all incinerated by a Red magic bolt just as the transaction was to have been completed. Accusations flew and tempers flared. Charms of power were
stolen and stolen again. The Academy of Conjuration was burned to the ground. Chaos reigned and ordinary humans hid in their cellars. A full-scale battle developed between the so-called Free Sorcerers Guild and the more conservative Brothers of the Colors. The end result was a lot of dead young Sorcerers and a blood compromise on the question of exclusivity of powers, charms and spells. Most of the magical beings managed to remain unavailable throughout the battle due to the cancelling effect of the many conflicting spells and summonses.


**[18.41]** STARTING FORCES

**Each Player:**   
One Three-Color Sorcerer   
Nine One-color Sorcerers (three in each of the colors of the Three-color Sorcerer)   
One (8) fortress   
Two (4) fortresses   
Three (2) fortresses   
Seven human infantry   
Deployment: as per 16.0   .

**[18.42]** VICTORY CONDITIONS   
As per 15.0, excluding the Points for Gray cities. Victory is judged when all White hexes have been converted, or at the end of ten Game-Turns, whichever comes first.   

SPECIAL RULES:

**[18.43]** **No** magical units may be conjured; vortexes may be conjured. All Sorcerers may teleport and fling bolts.   

**[18.44]** **Determining the Colors:**   
One Player should pick a three-color Sorcerer at random; the other Player gets the three-color Sorcerer of the remaining colors. The Players determine the color of the fortresses secretly with the limitation that each of their colors must have two fortresses.   

**[18.45] Multi-Player Variant:**   
Substitute two-color Sorcerers for the three-color Sorcerers (leaving six one-color Sorcerers per Player).
