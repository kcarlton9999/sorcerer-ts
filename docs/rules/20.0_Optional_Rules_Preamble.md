# Sorcerer Documentation

## [20.0] OPTIONAL RULES (Preamble)

The following sections give the Players Optional Rules that they may add to any of the various Scenarios as they deem fit and wherever they seem applicable. Some, all or none of them may be used; the Players are advised that some of the Optional Rules will distort the play of some Scenarios.

> Editor's Note: <br>
> We use the term "preamble" to distinguish the two sections that make up Section 20. The original documentation just has a section called OPTIONAL RULES but does not denote a section number. We impose a numbered section to make bookkeeping easier.
